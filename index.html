Mingxin Wu

I am a graduate student in the Department of Astronomy at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), mentored by Prof. Ning Jiang in Prof. Tinggui Wang's group. My work focuses on time-domain astronomy and transient especially tidal disruption events (TDEs). I am also a member of the Wide Field Survey Telescope (WFST) science team, where I use WFST data to search for and study transient phenomena.

Research Interests

01

TDE Searching

WFST, with its wide survey area, high cadence, deep sensitivity, and unique $u$-band coverage, is a powerful facility for discovering TDEs. I use WFST data to search for TDEs, especially low-luminosity and high-redshift events, with the goal of building a more complete census of the TDE population.

02

TDE Dust Echos

Dust surrounding TDEs can reprocess radiation from the central engine, producing delayed infrared emission. This infrared echo provides a valuable probe of the circumnuclear environment and offers insight into the physical processes powering TDEs.

03

TDE Coronal Emission Lines

TDEs occurring in gas-rich environments may produce strong coronal lines. Studying such events can help us understand the emission processes of their optical/UV, X-ray, and coronal-line radiation, and further constrain the distribution and physical properties of gas around the black hole.
