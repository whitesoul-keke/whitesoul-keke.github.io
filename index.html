Research Interests

01

TDE Searching

WFST, with its wide survey area, high cadence, deep sensitivity, and unique $u$-band coverage, is a powerful facility for discovering tidal disruption events (TDEs). I use WFST data to search for TDEs, especially low-luminosity and high-redshift events, with the goal of building a more complete census of the TDE population.
